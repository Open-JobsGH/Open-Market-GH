Return & Refund Policy

Returns & Refunds

What's eligible, and how refunds are processed

Order Cancellation

You may cancel an eligible order within 24 hours of placing it, as long as it hasn't entered processing yet. Once an order begins processing, packing, or preparation for dispatch, it can no longer be cancelled — even if the 24-hour window hasn't expired. Any countdown shown on your order is a convenience only, not a guarantee.

Eligible Returns

A return request may be submitted where the wrong product was delivered, the product arrived damaged or defective, the product significantly differs from its description, or an item is missing from the order. Please inspect your order as soon as it arrives.

Returned products should be in their original condition wherever reasonably possible, including original packaging, accessories, manuals and any promotional items supplied with the purchase. We may ask for photos or videos to support a return request.

What Cannot Be Returned

For hygiene and safety reasons, some items are not eligible for return, including:

  • Hygiene and personal care products that have been opened or used
  • Opened consumables (food, beverages, supplements)
  • Underwear and swimwear
  • Items marked as non-returnable on the product page

We may also refuse a return where a product shows signs of misuse, intentional damage, alteration, or wear inconsistent with reasonable inspection.

How to Request a Return

Open the order under My Orders and start a return request, or reach out through the Support Center with your Order ID and the reason for the return. Our team will review your request and let you know if it's approved.

How Refunds Work

Once your return or refund request has been reviewed and approved, your refund is processed back to your original payment method wherever possible. Approved refunds are generally issued within 10 working days after the review is complete. Delivery charges and processing fees may not be refundable unless the error was ours.

Exchanges

Where stock allows, we may offer an exchange instead of a refund. Whether an exchange or refund is the better fit depends on product availability, product condition, and the nature of the issue.

Unavailable or Cancelled Items

If a product you ordered becomes unavailable before it ships, we'll contact you directly and offer a suitable solution, including a full refund where applicable.
