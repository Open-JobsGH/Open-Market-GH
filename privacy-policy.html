1. Introduction

Welcome to Open Market. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Open Market collects, uses, stores, protects and shares your personal information when you use our Platform.

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, registered users and customers who access or use the Open Market website and any related services.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the Platform.

2. Who We Are

Open Market is a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) e-commerce platform operating in Ghana. Open Market is responsible for determining how and why your personal information is collected and processed in connection with the services provided through the Platform.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Open Market, we may collect the following categories of information.

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted form)
  • Profile picture (if uploaded)
Contact Information

When placing an order or contacting customer support, we may collect:

  • Delivery address
  • Town
  • Region
  • Telephone number
  • Alternative contact information where provided
Order Information

When you purchase products, we may collect:

  • Order history
  • Products purchased
  • Product variants selected
  • Quantity ordered
  • Delivery method selected
  • Pickup location (where applicable)
  • Payment status
  • Order status
  • Order ID
Payment Information

When making purchases, payment-related information may be processed through our authorised payment partners. Open Market does not intentionally store complete payment card numbers or sensitive payment credentials on its own servers unless required for lawful operational purposes.

Device Information

We may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device type
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • IP address
  • Language settings
  • Time zone
  • Device identifiers
Usage Information

We may collect information about how you use the Platform, including pages visited, products viewed, search history, time spent on pages, click activity, wishlist activity and cart activity. This information helps us improve the Platform and customer experience.

4. Information You Voluntarily Provide

You may voluntarily provide additional information when contacting customer support, reporting bugs, reporting poor customer service, reporting products, submitting feature requests, leaving product reviews, or participating in surveys or promotions.

5. How We Use Your Information

Open Market uses your information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Processing orders
  • Delivering purchased products
  • Managing Self Pickup orders
  • Providing customer support
  • Responding to complaints
  • Improving our products and services
  • Detecting fraud
  • Maintaining Platform security
  • Sending important account notifications
  • Processing refunds
  • Managing warranties
  • Improving search results and recommendations
  • Complying with legal obligations

We will only use your information where we have a lawful reason to do so.

6. Delivery Information

Where Home Delivery is selected, your delivery address is used solely for the purpose of delivering your order. Where Self Pickup is selected, your Order ID and registered name may be used to verify your identity before your order is released.

7. Customer Support

Information submitted to Customer Support may be retained to resolve disputes, improve service quality, train support personnel, investigate complaints, and prevent abuse of our services.

8. Cookies

Open Market may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, improve website performance, analyse visitor behaviour, enhance security, and personalise your experience.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain Platform features.

9. Analytics

Open Market may use analytical tools to better understand how customers use the Platform. These tools may collect anonymous or aggregated information regarding popular products, frequently searched items, device types and general browsing behaviour. This information is used to improve our services and customer experience.

10. Communications

We may contact you regarding orders, delivery updates, pickup notifications, account security, password resets, important policy updates, and customer support responses.

Where permitted by law, we may also send promotional communications. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time without affecting transactional communications.

11. Sharing of Information

Open Market does not sell your personal information. We may share information only where necessary with payment service providers, delivery partners, pickup agents, technology service providers, professional advisers, and government authorities where legally required.

Each recipient is expected to use your information only for the purpose for which it was provided.

12. Data Security

Open Market takes reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including encryption, secure servers, access controls, authentication systems, monitoring for suspicious activity, and regular security updates.

While we take reasonable precautions, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users also play an important role by keeping their passwords confidential.

13. Data Retention

Open Market retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to complete transactions, provide customer support, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain business records.

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

14. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, update your account details, delete your account, request deletion of certain personal information where legally permissible, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and object to certain processing activities.

Some requests may be limited where Open Market must retain information for legal or operational reasons.

15. Children's Privacy

Open Market is not intended for use by children who cannot legally enter into contracts under applicable law without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately from a child, reasonable steps will be taken to remove that information where required by law.

16. Third-Party Services

The Platform may contain links or integrations with third-party services. Open Market is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. Customers are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those services separately.

17. International Data Transfers

Where it becomes necessary to use service providers located outside Ghana, Open Market will take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect personal information.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Open Market may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legislation, improvements to our services, security enhancements, and operational requirements.

Where significant changes are made, reasonable efforts will be made to notify users through the Platform or by email. Continued use of the Platform after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

19. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, you may contact Open Market through the Support Center available on the Platform.

Additional contact information, including our official support email, telephone number and business address, may be published on the Platform before launch.
