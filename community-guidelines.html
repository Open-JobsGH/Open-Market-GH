Community Guidelines

Be Respectful

Treat our support team and fellow shoppers with respect. Harassment, hate speech, or abusive behaviour of any kind is not tolerated.

Fair Use of the Platform

Every product on Open Market is sold directly by us — please don't attempt fraudulent orders, payment abuse, or misuse of promotions and returns.

Honest Reviews

Reviews should reflect your genuine experience with a product. Fake or incentivised reviews go against the spirit of an open, trustworthy store.

These guidelines help keep Open Market safe and fair.
